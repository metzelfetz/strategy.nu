id: approach
title: Approach
order: 2
intro: Four steps, from the first conversation to execution.
steps:
  - title: Coffee.
    subtitle: Discovery meeting
    body: An informal first meeting – no slides, no sales pitch. We talk about your situation, your goals and what is really going on, and find out whether there is a fit.
  - title: Clarity.
    subtitle: Value analysis
    body: Before anything is agreed, we run a short analysis of where we can add real value. If we are not the right partner, we say so. If we are, you know exactly why.
  - title: Plan.
    subtitle: Strategy workshop
    body: One full day. We go through your last three balance sheets, your market, your competitors and your sector to answer the basic question – continue or exit? If continuing makes sense, we work out which growth options, efficiency gains, pivots and partnerships are worth pursuing. You leave with priorities, quick wins and a practical roadmap.
  - title: Do.
    subtitle: Execution support
    body: A plan only counts once it is carried out. We stay on as on-demand advisors or as members of your advisory board, with project management included, and bring in partners from our network where they are needed. From startups we borrow short cycles, close contact with customers and quick decisions – and apply them to established businesses.
